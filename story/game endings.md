Es gibt 5 mögliche Spielaußgänge:

**1. Rescue — Hope dominant, Knowledge low-mid**  
Optimism carried ending.
Pessimist saved, redeemed, or reconciled with.
Player didn't chase every thread: world's deeper questions stay unresolved. Bittersweet-happy — earned, but incomplete.


**2. Truth — Doubt dominant, Knowledge low-mid**  
Doubt wins
Pessimist's grim read on the world holds up
Something true but painful is confirmed.
Sad, but honest: "hope" the optimist clung doesn't survive contact with reality yet

**3. Open — Chaos dominant, Knowledge low-mid**  
Nothing resolves cleanly, or is fully lost either
Uncertainty-with-a-door-left-open — chaos did not destro/save anything
Refuse to let the story close
"maybe things get better"

**4. The Only Truth — Knowledge maxed (tone-independent)**  
Triggered not by which tone dominates, but player having pursued _everything_ — every investigation, every uncomfortable question, regardless of whether they leaned optimistic, pessimistic, or chaotic along the way. "No white points on the map" is a great literal hook: this is the completionist/true ending, and it should feel different in _kind_, not just in degree, from 1–3.

**5. Secret — Chaos at extreme, gated by a threshold not a dominance**  
This is the one that shouldn't trigger from "leaning chaotic a lot" — it should require **Chaos maxed while the other two are near-zero**, i.e., the character stopped engaging with hope or doubt entirely. I'd suggest your three variants aren't three separate endings but **three flavors of the same gate**, decided by whichever secondary stat was closer to zero-crossing at the trigger point:

- Chaos max + Doubt was the last thing to collapse → **self-destruction** (the story refuses to continue — a real "game over" that isn't a fail-state, it's an ending)
- Chaos max + both others already near-zero for a while → **total breakdown** (nothing left to resolve — bleakest variant)
- Chaos max + Hope was the last thing to collapse → **rebirth from ruin** (destruction as a precondition for something new, not an end)
